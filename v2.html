Norrbin & Vane Bookbinders

Cloth-and-board rebinding, marbled endpapers, and letterpress stationery — one bench, one press, no shortcuts.

What we do

We rebind paperbacks in buckram cloth, repair cracked spines on family bibles, and hand-marble endpapers in small batches. Everything is stitched, not glued — signatures sewn on tapes, boards cased in by hand.
